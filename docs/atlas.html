How we measure realtime video AI

Live sessions against live products — every delivered frame recorded, every failure attributed, every judgment blinded. Lucy 2.5 wins every profile; the gap collapses from 46 points to 12.7 when the question becomes China-market streaming.

01 · Pipeline

One pipeline, five campaigns

Fixed, hashed stimulus goes in; a real streaming session runs; guards keep network sins out of product scores; four measurement families read the same capture; scores roll up through declared weights. Campaigns vary one axis at a time — duration mix (reliability), same-input pairs (quality), mid-stream instructions (editing), world prompts (generation), dual routing (platform tax).

02 · Instruments

Four measurement families, one capture

Each family is blind to something the others see — the design is the redundancy. When a computed number and the judge disagree, a human looks.

04 · Why trust it

The honesty machinery

Lens separationNative, aggregator-routed, and browser-captured results never share a table — a middleman's 700 ms is not the model's sin.
Failures get attributedProduct, network, or rig — with evidence. 356 Lucy runs were excluded as tunnel faults rather than counted against her.
Judges on a leashBlinded frames, forced schemas, hidden repeats, human agreement gates — and extreme calls get human eyes on the raw pixels.
Fixed stimulusInputs are hashed; a rerun streams bit-identical pixels.
Dated snapshotsServer builds are recorded; providers hot-deploy mid-week. Results are dated claims.
Self-appliedOur own mistakes are documented in-repo — including a retracted vendor bug report and a journal-loss incident.
